# RATBOT

## Large Language Models - the necessary evil of the 20's

I guess no matter how much you disagree with the use and misuse of AI, it is virtually impossible to escape the tentation of asking Chat GPT a few questions.

I am one of those people.

While everyone is streamlining their workspace and tooling with more AI integration, by having a direct link between their IDE and some LLM API that analyses a whole project in mere seconds, I refuse to let go of my beloved Vim. Not that Vim wouldn't be capable of performing the same task by means of a plug-in.

Behind my grumpy old man face, there's a reason: Like wearing shoes with more cushion, or getting a car and ditching the bicycle, I see myself very easily getting used to these tools and never going back to harder times. The problem with that is that, I learn better, understand better and interiorise better, if I'm part of the pipeline.

Let me explain: if I take notes, even if those notes don't survive, my brain is more likely to _retain_ the information I've just ingested. Taking notes is an extra step that makes me pay more attention to the information in question. The same applies if I read from one screen and type what I've read on a second screen. If I instead simply copy and paste, I can be sure I won't remember a thing ten minutes from now. In fact, by copy and paste, I won't even read the whole thing in the first place.

AI tools seem to take this concept to a higher level, where we are getting used to fast results, instant gratification, and with that, no real inspection of what's happening with our code and no persistent learning.

My use of Chat GPT within this project is to have a conversation with a more knowledgeable colleague, that can teach me techniques, the meaning of concepts that are new to me, and sometimes, speed up the process that required extensive google searches to compile the required information for the project, but the idea, the high level design, runs under my direction. Chat GPT also acts as an online course, where I start with something simple, such as a `hello world` example, followed by a combination of accepting some of its follow-up suggestions or, leading the conversation towards a premeditated result, but always in small steps, including an explanation.

It's always easy to fall into the patronising manner of Chat GPT or, if experimenting with other models, I'm sure they're all extremely capable of leading a human being to meeting the interests of the corporation behind that model -- in the near future, to receive advertisement in a novel and seamless way, perhaps.

Whatever you do, always remember to have a conversation, lead the project, gaslight the LLM when in doubt, and do some random alternate traditional google research every now and then! - Be patient; Learn!
